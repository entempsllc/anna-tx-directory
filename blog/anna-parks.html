Plan your park visit with official City sources

The City of Anna’s official directory lists Baldwin Park, Bryant Park, Geer Park, Henry Clay “Yank” Washington Park, John Flavel Greer Park, Johnson Park, Natural Springs Park, Sherley Heritage Park, and Slayter Creek Park, plus Slayter Creek’s splash pad, disc golf course, and trails.

The City notes that its directory includes parks that are currently in development or planned for future development. Before visiting, confirm current access, hours, and amenities on the official detail page rather than relying on a name alone.
